## The Evolving Landscape of Tariffs & Trade Policy in 2026

### Supreme Court's Impact on Tariff Authority

The most significant recent development is the Supreme Court's decision that has fundamentally altered the executive branch's unilateral power to impose tariffs. As reported by CNBC, this ruling has "upended tariff authority," creating a wave of questions regarding the legality and implementation of future trade actions. While former President Trump insists existing trade deals remain safe, "partners aren’t so sure," leading to a period of flux and heightened scrutiny (CNBC). The New York Times further notes that the administration has been "scrambl[ing] to pick up the pieces of broken tariffs," highlighting the real-world implications of this legal challenge on established trade policies.

This legal shift means that any future attempts to impose or modify tariffs, particularly under statutes like Section 301 or 232, will likely face increased legal hurdles and potential delays. For importers, this translates into a volatile environment where the rules of engagement are subject to ongoing reinterpretation and challenge.
